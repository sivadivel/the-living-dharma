# Glossary of Buddhist Terms

Organized by language and theme. Add terms as you encounter them.

## Pali Terms

| Term | Pronunciation | Meaning | Context |
|------|--------------|---------|---------|
| Dukkha | DOOK-kha | Suffering, unsatisfactoriness | First Noble Truth |
| Anicca | ah-NI-cha | Impermanence | Three characteristics |
| Anattā | ah-NAT-tah | Non-self | Three characteristics |
| Mettā | MET-tah | Loving-kindness | Brahmavihāras |
| Sati | SAH-tee | Mindfulness, awareness | Eightfold Path |
| Samādhi | sah-MAH-dee | Concentration, meditative absorption | Eightfold Path |
| Pañña | PAHN-ya | Wisdom | Eightfold Path |
| Nibbāna | nih-BAH-nah | Liberation (Pali form of Nirvāna) | Goal of practice |
| Dhamma | DAH-mah | Teaching; phenomena; truth | Core concept |
| Sangha | SANG-ha | Community of practitioners | Three Jewels |
